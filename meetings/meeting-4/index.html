Part 2 · Jobs · This is about us

New grads are struggling. Is AI the reason?

Recent grads (22–27): 5.7% unemployed · Everyone: 4.1%

Yes, it’s AI

Stanford: workers 22–25 in AI-exposed jobs like software and marketing are down 16% since late 2022.

47% of recent grads say AI already changed hiring in their field.

No, it’s something else

Companies spending the most on AI grew entry-level hiring 12% (21,000+ firms).

Junior hiring started falling about six months before ChatGPT came out.

NPR, Aug 19, 2026 (NY Fed, Stanford, Ramp/Revelio, ZipRecruiter data)
