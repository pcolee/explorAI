Let’s argue about AI

Today is about the open questions. Each topic is one slide: the statement or question, a few facts on notes, and follow-up questions that appear one per press. The slides carry the facts, so don't read them out. Ask the question in quotes, then listen. Pink Do notes are cues. The Bridge line leads into the next slide. On the laptop, S opens presenter view and M gives a phone remote.
