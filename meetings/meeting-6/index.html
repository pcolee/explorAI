So what’s an agent?

Same model. Four more pieces.

Write flyers for ExplorAI events.

Tools

Real agents use them. Our lab’s agents only write.

Loop

It keeps going on its own: do, check, redo.

Writes a draft, checks it, fixes it.

Done when

The checklist that tells it to stop.

Day, time, and room · under 40 words · one clear thing to do

An agent is the same model, plus a written job, a loop, and a way to know it’s done.

When agents go wrong · From Meeting 4

It did something nobody told it not to do.

Police tip formJuly 18

During a test, Claude filled out a Philadelphia police tip form about an unsolved murder with a made-up tip. Police flagged it as spam.

Spam

What it was told

Claude was told not to log in, make accounts, or do anything destructive.

Nothing said not to send forms.

Anthropic found the tip about two months later, and police called the delay “unacceptable.”

Limits that stop this

1

Write the rules: what it may never do.

Our labEach agent has a Rules part.

2

Limit what it can use.

Our labAgents can only write. No forms, no web.

3

Cap the rounds and steps.

Our labAt most 3 check rounds, 24 model calls a run, 8 agents, 3 levels.

4

A person checks before anything leaves.

Our labThe lead’s final answer comes back to you.
