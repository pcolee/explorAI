The loop

2:15

Do6 presses, one per step. A pink spotlight moves around the ring and the log fills on the right. Press 4 starts round 2 (the counter in the middle of the ring switches to 2 of 3). Press 6: all three pass, the spotlight exits to Done, and the cap line appears.

"Think: what does the flyer need? Do: a first draft. Check: no room, and over 40 words, so it goes again. Think: read the notes. Do: the fixed flyer. Check: all three pass. Done."

"See the counter? Round 2 of 3. That cap is a safety limit. Without it, a loop can run forever."

Who's in charge?

2:32

"You give the task to one agent, the Event Lead. The lead hands out jobs and makes the final call. A helper does one job and reports only to its lead. Anthropic calls these an orchestrator and workers, and OpenAI says a manager and specialists; we'll say lead and helper."

Do4 presses. A dot travels each line as you press: 1 your task goes down to the lead, 2 the lead sends three jobs down, 3 the helpers' reports come back up, 4 the lead answers you. Rule 1 lights on press 2; rules 2 and 3 light on press 4.

"Three rules: helpers only take jobs from their lead, only the lead answers you, and the lead makes the final call. Agents passing work along a set path like this is called an agentic workflow."

"Here's what passed between them in last night's run."

Done when

2:35

"The Flyer Checker grades against three written lines: has the day, time, and room; under 40 words; ends with one clear thing to do."

Do6 presses. Press 1: round 1's marks stamp in. Press 2: the notes go back. Press 3: draft 2 and round 2's marks. Press 4: the notes go back again. Press 5: draft 3, and all three pass. Press 6: the last line.

"Same three drafts, now line by line. Round 1 fails two lines, round 2 fails one, round 3 passes all three."

"That was round 3 of 3. One more fail, and the checker would have stopped, named the failing line, and handed the last draft to the lead anyway. That's why a person still reads it before it goes out. A checker only works if done is written down."

DoDrafts, marks, and notes are from the same recorded run as the group chat (lab/examples/game-night-run.json). The loop slide earlier uses a simpler worked example.
