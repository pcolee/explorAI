So what's an agent?

2:16

"Same model, Gemini here. The model is the AI itself."

DoPress once per piece.

"Instructions: its job, written once. Tools: things it can use besides writing, like a web search or a calculator. Our lab's agents only write. Loop: it keeps going on its own, do, check, redo, without you typing each step. Done when: the checklist that tells it to stop."

DoPress for the definition.

"An agent is the same model, plus a written job, a loop, and a way to know it's done."

"Let's watch that loop run once."

11

The loop

2:18

DoPress once per step. The counter switches to round 2 on the fourth press.

"Think: what does a flyer need? Do: a first draft. Check: too long, and no room. So the notes go back. Do again: the fixed flyer. Check: all three pass. Done."

"See the counter? Round 1 of 3. That cap is a guardrail. Without it, a loop can run forever."

"Enough watching. Time to make one."

Who's in charge?

2:33

"You give the task to one agent, the Event Lead. The lead hands out jobs and makes the final call. A helper does one job and reports only to its lead. Other tools call these an orchestrator and sub-agents; we'll say lead and helper."

DoPress once per step: your task goes to the lead, the lead hands out jobs, helpers report back, the lead answers you.

"Three rules: helpers only take jobs from their lead, only the lead answers you, and the lead makes the final call. Agents passing work along a set path like this is called an agentic workflow."

"So what are they actually saying to each other?"
