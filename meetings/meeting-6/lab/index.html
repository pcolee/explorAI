    Use your own Gemini key

    With a free key, real Gemini models do the work. Without one, practice mode plays recorded replies.

    1. Open Google AI Studio's API keys page and sign in with a personal Google account.
    2. New accounts get a key made for them. To make another, press Create API key.
    3. Copy the key and paste it here.

    Without this box, the key is forgotten when you close the tab.

    • Your key goes only to Google. It is never in a share link.
    • On the free tier, Google may use what you type to improve its products, and people may read it. Don't paste anything private.
    • Google's terms say you must be 18 or older to use the Gemini API. Under 18? Use practice mode.
