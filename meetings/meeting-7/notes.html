One standard way for AI to connect to apps and tools

2:12

"MCP stands for Model Context Protocol. It's one standard way for AI to connect to apps and tools."

Do3 presses, one word of the name each.

"Model: the AI itself, like Claude."

"Context: what it can read right now. That's the context window from Meeting 3."

"Protocol: rules both sides agree on, so they can talk to each other."

DoIf asked: Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, released MCP in November 2024 as an open standard, so other companies can use it too. OpenAI and Google support it.

"Here's a way to picture it."

7

MCP is like USB for AI

2:14

"MCP is like USB for AI. Before USB, every device needed its own cable."

Do3 presses. Press 1: before.

"Three AI apps and three apps you use. Without a standard, every pair needs its own connection. That's nine, each one built separately."

DoPress 2: with MCP.

"With MCP, every app gets the same plug. Each side builds it once, and they all fit together."

DoPress 3: the line.

"So any AI that supports MCP can use any app that supports it."

"So what are the pieces in that picture?"

8

Three parts work together

2:16

Do4 presses, one part each, then the line.

"The host, or client, is the AI app you use, like Claude or Cursor."

"The server is the connector for one app: Google Drive, GitHub, Notion. Each app has its own."

"Tools are the actions a server offers: search files, read a doc, list events."

"Claude calls a tool, the server does it in the app, and the result comes back to Claude."

"You've seen where that happens before."
