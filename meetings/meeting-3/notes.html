Everything goes into one window

2:36–2:40

"Every time you hit send, the app builds one block of text: hidden instructions, any files, the whole chat, then your new message."

"It writes the reply one token at a time, each new token goes back into the window before the next."

"That's the only thing it can see. Even memory features are just notes the app slips in here."

DoPress once per block, top to bottom.

20

Five things that make an answer better

2:44–2:46

"This is the recipe. Five things to put in the window before you hit send."

"Number one is the big one — paste the actual material. The rubric, the job posting, the syllabus, the email thread. People skip this and wonder why the answer is generic. The AI can only work with what's in the window."

"Two: who are you, or who should the AI be? A first-year student, a hiring manager, an expert in X? It shapes the whole tone."

"Three: what do you actually want? Be specific. 'Help me with this email' is vague. 'Write a two-sentence reply declining this meeting' is not."

"Four: why — the situation. What's the context? Missed a lab because of a family emergency. Applying with no experience yet. That why changes everything."

"Five: how. Format, length, tone — in numbers, not vibes. 'Short' means nothing. 'Under 100 words, no bullet points' is a constraint."

DoPress once per card to reveal 1–5. Ask: which one are you most likely to skip?

"Let's look at what this looks like in practice."

21

Vague vs. Vivid

2:46–2:48

"Here's the same task: write an email to your professor."

"Vague version: the AI has to guess everything. Which professor, what course, what happened, what tone, how long."

"Vivid version: Who is a student in Intro to Python. What is a short polite email to Professor Lee. Why is a missed lab from a family emergency. How is under 100 words."

"Four answers instead of zero. One much better result."

DoSmall-room option: let someone call out a detail to add to the vague version and rerun it live.

"Now — your turn. Same task as before, the recipe this time."
