Gemini 4 Argon

What it is

Google DeepMind’s new flagship model, announced Sept 30, 2026. Built for long, multi-step work — it plans, takes many actions, and keeps track of the whole task.

Who it’s for right now

Vetted cyber defenders in Google’s Fairwind Program — governments, critical infrastructure, major platforms. API and Ultra subscribers are next. No date for everyone else.

Why it’s notable

Very strong at long context: huge documents, codebases, long videos. It reportedly caught a critical bug in hospital software that earlier models missed.

Every Argon headline measures it in tokens: up to a million tokens per response, priced per million tokens.

AI Context & Prompting

What goes
in the window

And how to fill it well.

The context window

Everything goes into one window

Every time you hit send, the app packs all of this into the window, and the model reads it from the top.

It has no other memory. Even "memory" features work by adding notes to the window.

Instructions from the appRules the company wrote. You usually never see them.
Files and pasted materialYour notes, a PDF, a syllabus
The whole chat so farEvery message, both sides, sent again
Your new messageThe part you think of as "the prompt"
Its reply, one token at a timeEach new token joins the window before the next one is predicted

ExplorAI · Meeting 3

Why it "forgets"

A tiny window, one chat

YouAIFell out of the window3 · 11 = message 3, 11 tokens

Message 1 said "Friday." At turn 4 it’s gone, so the model has to guess.

Pretend window: tokens. Real ones hold far more, and some apps summarize old messages instead of dropping them. Same idea, just later.

The rule

It can’t read what you didn’t put in the window.

What to put in the window

Five things that make an answer better

0

The material

Paste the real thing: the rubric, the syllabus, the job post. This is the part people skip, and it matters most.

1

Who

Who you are and what role you’re writing from

2

What

Exactly what you need — specific, not vague

3

Why

What this is for and the context around it

4

How

Limits in numbers: "150 words, 3 bullets," not "short"

ExplorAI · Meeting 3

Vague vs. Vivid

Same task. Very different context.

Vague

"Write an email to my professor."

The AI has to guess: which professor? what course? what happened? what tone? what length? Any answer is a guess.

Vivid

"I’m a student in Intro to Python. I missed Tuesday’s lab because of a family emergency. Write a short, polite email to Professor Lee asking if I can make it up this week. Keep it under 100 words."

Every sentence answers a question the AI would’ve guessed wrong.

What changed? Who + What + Why + How. Four rules, one much better result.
